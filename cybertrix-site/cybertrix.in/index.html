What we do

Practical skills,
not slide decks.

Most cybersecurity courses are just video lectures. At Cybertrix.in, every topic comes with a lab environment where you practice on real tools — Burp Suite, Metasploit, Nmap, Wireshark, Volatility — the same stack you'd use on the job.

01
Hands-on labs with every module

03

Our content maps to CEH, OSCP, eJPT, and CompTIA Security+ so your learning transfers directly to certification prep.

Discord access, resume reviews, mock interviews, and referrals to security teams actively hiring.
